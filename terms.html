1. Service and consent

Pamamap lets you share your location with members of rooms you join and receive alerts when members arrive at or leave saved places. Only share your own location from your own device. Do not track anyone without their consent. You can disable sharing with Ghost Mode in the app or revoke location permission in your device settings.

2. Subscriptions and trial

  • Vietnam prices: VND 39,000/month or VND 429,000/year. The price, currency, taxes, billing period and offer shown by the store before purchase apply to your transaction.
  • A 3-day trial is available to eligible users when the store displays the offer. Eligibility is determined by the store. Unless cancelled in time, the trial becomes a paid subscription and renews automatically.
  • Google Play or Apple processes payments. Subscriptions renew until cancelled. Deleting the app or your Pamamap account does not cancel a subscription.
  • Cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. For Apple, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Request refunds through the store under its policies. If access expires, the app may display a lock screen. You can still manage subscriptions in the store and revoke location permission in device settings. If you cannot reach in-app Settings to delete your account, use the support deletion-request page. If payment is not reflected, use Restore purchases or contact support.

3. One account, one device

An account may have one valid device at a time. Signing in on a new device may replace the previous session. Access is tied to your Pamamap account; use that same account on another device. Do not share an account among multiple people.

4. Account and data

Protect your sign-in information and take responsibility for the content you enter. See the Privacy Policy and account deletion page.

5. Prohibited conduct

No covert tracking, impersonation, harassment, unlawful content, system interference or bypassing payment and device limits. Do not give room invitation links or codes to people you do not want to allow access.

6. Accuracy and liability

Location and alerts depend on GPS, networks, devices and operating systems and may be delayed or inaccurate. Pamamap is not an emergency service. To the extent permitted by law, the liability cap is the amount paid during the preceding 12 months. This does not exclude mandatory consumer rights.

7. Changes and governing law

Updates will be posted with an effective date. Price changes follow store notice and renewal rules. The governing law is Vietnamese law; mandatory rights under the laws of your place of residence remain protected.
